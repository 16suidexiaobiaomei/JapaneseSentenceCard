Support

Answers to common questions, and how to reach us for anything else.

How do I reset my password?

On the login screen, log in with your email as usual — if you've forgotten your password, contact us below and we can help you regain access.

How do I delete my account and data?

Open the app, go to your Profile (tap your avatar), and choose Delete account. This immediately and permanently removes your account and everything in it — cards, tags, recordings, and review history.

Romaji isn't generating for a sentence

Romaji generation needs an internet connection. If you're offline, it's skipped and you can type romaji manually — it'll still generate automatically the next time you're online and edit the card.

My voice recording sounds distorted

If this happens, please let us know which device and iOS version you're on — this helps us track down audio issues specific to certain hardware.

Still need help?

Email us at alice.jee@outlook.com and we'll get back to you.
