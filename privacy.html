Privacy Policy

Last updated September 12, 2026

Japanese Sentence Card ("the app") is a flashcard app for studying Japanese sentences. This page explains what information the app collects, why, and how you can remove it.

What we collect

  • Account information: the email address and password you sign up with. Passwords are handled entirely by our authentication provider (Supabase) and are never visible to us in plain text.
  • Your cards: the Japanese sentences, translations, romaji, and tags you create.
  • Voice recordings: if you choose to record your own pronunciation for a card, that audio is stored as part of that card's data.
  • Study activity: review history and statistics (such as streaks and cards reviewed) used to power spaced-repetition scheduling and the activity view.

We do not collect location data, contacts, or any information beyond what's listed above. The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

How it's used

Your information is used solely to operate the app for you: authenticating your account, syncing your cards and study history across your devices, and generating romaji for sentences you enter (processed through our own backend function, not a third party).

Where it's stored

Data is stored with our infrastructure providers, Supabase (database and authentication) and Vercel (hosting). Row-level security ensures your data is only ever accessible to your own account. We do not sell or share your data with advertisers or other third parties.

Your control over your data

You can permanently delete your account at any time from within the app (Profile → Delete account). This immediately and permanently removes your account and all associated data — cards, tags, recordings, and review history — and cannot be undone.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to alice.jee@outlook.com.
